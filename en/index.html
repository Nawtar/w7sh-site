Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything in W7SH is free: no subscription, no trial that runs out, no in-app purchases, and no cap on your workouts or plans.

Are there ads?

No. No ads, and no tracking across apps and websites.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every set is saved on your phone the moment you tick it, even with no signal in the gym, and syncs when you are back online.

What is the flame?

From version 1.3: every day you reach ten thousand steps keeps your flame lit, and the days in a row show next to your name. Each week in a row earns a shield that saves your flame on a missed day; you hold up to two.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone first, with a copy on servers in Frankfurt, Germany, so you can reach it from your devices. The details are in the privacy policy.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes. Export a CSV from Strong, Hevy or FitNotes, then import it from Account.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. W7SH is made for iPhone.
