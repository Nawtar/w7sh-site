Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Logging, routines, history and charts are free with no time limit and no caps. Plus is optional and adds programs and tools.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is saved on your phone first and syncs when you are back online.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Not yet. Importing from a CSV file is on our list.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone, with a copy on servers in Frankfurt, Germany during the beta. Export or delete it whenever you like. The details are in the privacy policy.

How do I cancel Plus?

Settings, "Manage subscription", or your App Store subscriptions on your iPhone. One tap, and Plus stays until the end of the period you paid for.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. iPhone comes first.
