Privacy policy

Pre-launch draft, pending legal review.

In short

W7SH keeps your training so you can reach it from any device. No ads, no tracking across apps and websites, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Your email address to sign in, or what Apple shares when you sign in with Apple (possibly a private relay address, and your name the first time only), or what Google shares when you continue with Google (your email, name and account picture). If your Google account's email is the email of your W7SH account, it opens that same account.
  • What you log: workouts, sets, weights, reps, routines, your own exercises and body measurements such as bodyweight. We treat this as sensitive health data.
  • Your settings: language, digit style, weight unit and time zone.
  • If you join the leaderboards (optional, off by default): the nickname you choose, your country and city (found from your location once, when you tap; the coordinates are not kept), and your steps and active energy for each day as Apple Health totals them. Other W7SH users see them with your nickname and country only. When you leave the boards, all of it is deleted at once.
  • If you set up a public profile (optional): your username and shown name, who you follow and who follows you, the workouts you choose to share (a summary your phone makes: title, length, volume, exercises and best sets, with your caption), likes, the monthly challenges you join and your score, the programs you publish and your program ratings. Your profile is private unless you make it public, and only people you accept see your posts.
  • Reports you send, and who you have blocked, so we can protect the community and review reports.
  • If you connect WHOOP (optional): your WHOOP access keys (encrypted) and, for each day, the summary WHOOP shares: recovery score, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, skin temperature, sleep (performance, hours, consistency, efficiency), day strain, energy and heart rate. W7SH only reads from WHOOP and never writes to it. You can disconnect at any time in the app; deleting your account deletes all of it.
  • Short-lived technical logs at our providers, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifier and no tracking. We never keep your location coordinates. Apple Health data stays on your phone and is never used for ads, except your daily steps and active energy if you join the leaderboards.
  • If you turn on saving workouts to Apple Health, your phone writes each workout you finish (its type, start and end only) to Health on your device. It does not pass through our servers.
  • CSV files you import from other apps are read on your phone and not uploaded. Share cards are drawn on your phone.
  • Gym check-in codes stay on your phone: never synced, not in your export, deleted with your account.
  • Progress photos stay on your phone (and in your iPhone backup, if you use one): never uploaded to our servers or synced, deleted with your account. Only what you choose to share leaves it.
  • If you use your data with an AI assistant on your computer (W7SH's small server), it reads your export file there and sends nothing to us.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase: database and sign-in, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Resend: sends your sign-in code.
  • Google: only if you continue with Google, to confirm who you are when you sign in.
  • Cloudflare: delivers exercise videos (needs none of your personal data).
  • WHOOP: only if you connect your account, to read the daily summary above.
  • Any new provider, such as crash reporting, is added here before it is used.

Where it is kept

On your phone first, with a copy on servers in Germany (EU). Under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law we ask for your explicit consent to process your health data and to transfer it outside the Kingdom before we store it.

Your rights

  • Access and copy: export all your data from Settings as CSV or JSON.
  • Correct: edit anything you logged at any time.
  • Delete: delete your account from Settings; it is removed from our servers at once, and from backups within their retention period.
  • Questions, or to use your rights: w7sh.app@gmail.com

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We announce changes here and in the app before they take effect. Last updated: 2026-10-14.
