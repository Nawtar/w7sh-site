Privacy policy

Pre-launch draft, pending legal review.

In short

W7SH keeps your training so you can reach it from any device. No ads, no tracking across apps and websites, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Your email address to sign in, or what Apple shares when you sign in with Apple (possibly a private relay address, and your name the first time only).
  • What you log: workouts, sets, weights, reps, routines, your own exercises and body measurements such as bodyweight. We treat this as sensitive health data.
  • Your settings: language, digit style, weight unit and time zone.
  • Your Plus subscription status if you subscribe, as Apple reports it through RevenueCat. We never see your card.
  • Short-lived technical logs at our providers, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service.

What we do not collect

No advertising identifier, no tracking, no location. Apple Health data, once the connection is added, stays on your phone and is never used for ads.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase: database and sign-in, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Resend: sends your sign-in code.
  • RevenueCat and Apple: subscriptions and in-app purchases.
  • Cloudflare: delivers exercise videos (needs none of your personal data).
  • Any new provider, such as crash reporting, is added here before it is used.

Where it is kept

On your phone first, with a copy on servers in Germany (EU) during the beta. Under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law we ask for your explicit consent to process your health data and to transfer it outside the Kingdom before we store it.

Your rights

  • Access and copy: export all your data from Settings as CSV or JSON.
  • Correct: edit anything you logged at any time.
  • Delete: delete your account from Settings; it is removed from our servers at once, and from backups within their retention period.
  • A contact address is being set up and will appear here soon. Until then you can export your data and delete your account from Settings in the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We announce changes here and in the app before they take effect. Last updated: 2026-10-08.
