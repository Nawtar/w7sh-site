Support

Contact

Email w7sh.app@gmail.com. We reply within two working days.

Delete your account

Settings → Delete account. Your account and everything you logged are deleted from every device. If you subscribe to Plus, also cancel it in your App Store subscriptions, because Apple does the billing.

Cancel Plus

Settings → Manage subscription, or your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Refunds

Apple decides refunds for App Store purchases. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com. If a child bought it by mistake, Family Sharing's Ask to Buy prevents it next time.

Export your data

Settings → Export my data, then CSV for your training log or JSON for everything.

Report and block

Tap any nickname on the leaderboards or challenges, or the menu of any profile, post or program, and choose Report or Block. The team reviews reports within 24 hours. The people you blocked are in Settings → Blocked people.

Import your history from another app

Profile → Import history. It takes CSV exports from Strong, Hevy and FitNotes, and skips workouts already in your history.
