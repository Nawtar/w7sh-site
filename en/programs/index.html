Programs

Programs the W7SH team wrote, in Arabic and English, for every level and setting. Follow them for free, with the next workout ready each day.

Strong Start

Beginner · 3 days a week · 8 weeks

Three full-body days a week, alternating two workouts. For your first months in the gym, or a comeback: a few basic lifts you learn well and build on.

Five-by-Five Foundation

Beginner · 3 days a week · 12 weeks

A simple strength plan: three lifts a day, five sets of five, three days a week. All about adding weight, little by little, to the big compound lifts.

Upper / Lower

Intermediate · 4 days a week · 10 weeks

Four days a week: two upper-body days and two lower-body days. One heavy, low-rep day and one higher-volume day for each, for strength and size together.

Push, Pull, Legs

Intermediate · 6 days a week · 12 weeks

Three days you run twice a week: push (chest, shoulders, triceps), pull (back, biceps) and legs. High training volume for lifters past the beginner stage.

Dumbbells at Home

Beginner · 3 days a week · 8 weeks

Three days a week with just a pair of dumbbells, and a bench if you have one. A full-body workout each day, for beginners and anyone who can't get to a gym.

Bodyweight Anywhere

Beginner · 3 days a week · 6 weeks

No equipment: a floor and a sturdy table for inverted rows. Two days you alternate three times a week, for general fitness, fat loss and travel.

Classic Five-Day Split

Advanced · 5 days a week · 12 weeks

Five days, one muscle group each: chest, back, legs, shoulders, arms. Lots of volume for each muscle once a week, for experienced lifters.

Glutes and Legs Focus

Intermediate · 4 days a week · 8 weeks

Four days: two hard days for glutes and legs, and two lighter upper-body days to keep you balanced.
