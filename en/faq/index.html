Questions and answers

What people ask most about W7SH. Didn't find yours? Write to us and we'll answer.

Price

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything in W7SH is free: no subscription, no trial that runs out, no in-app purchases, and no cap on your workouts or plans.

How can it be free?

W7SH is made and paid for by its maker. We don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and there is no paid version.

Are there ads?

No. No ads, and no tracking across apps and websites.

Training

Do I need a plan?

No. Start an empty workout and add what you like, save your own plans, or follow one of the W7SH programs written in Arabic and English.

Who writes the programs?

The W7SH programs are written by the W7SH team. The app also has programs members publish; those are their authors' responsibility.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every set is saved on your phone the moment you tick it, even with no signal in the gym, and syncs when you are back online.

An exercise isn't in the library

Add it as your own in seconds; custom exercises are unlimited. From version 1.2 you can also request it from the search screen, and the request reaches the W7SH team.

Does it do cardio?

Yes: by time and distance. From version 1.2 you can mark the part each set is (walk, jog, run, sprint, rest), and every timed set has a timer.

Kilograms or pounds?

Both, and you can switch any time in Settings. W7SH stores weights in kilograms and shows them in your unit.

Your data and privacy

Where is my data kept?

On your phone first, with a copy on servers in Frankfurt, Germany, so you can reach it from your devices. The details are in the privacy policy.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. Export everything you logged to CSV or JSON whenever you like, and delete your account and all its data from inside the app.

And my progress photos and gym check-in code?

They stay on your phone only: never synced, never in the export, and deleted with your account.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes. Export a CSV from Strong, Hevy or FitNotes, then import it from Account.

iPhone, Apple Watch and Apple Health

Is it on Android?

Not yet. W7SH is made for iPhone.

Does it work with Apple Watch?

From version 1.2: your wrist shows the workout in progress, the next set and the rest, and you tick sets from the watch. The watch shows what is on your iPhone, so keep the phone nearby.

What does it save to Apple Health?

Each finished workout is saved once, with its start and end and no calories, so nothing is counted twice with your watch. From version 1.2 the workout carries its volume too, and W7SH can read your body weight from Apple Health if you allow it.

Are there widgets?

The rest timer is on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. From version 1.2 there are Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets: the weekly goal, the next workout, the gym card, body weight and the latest personal best.

Is Arabic fully supported?

Yes. Right to left done properly, search that knows gym words like "بنش", weeks that start on Sunday, and Arabic digits if you want them. Pick the language in Settings › Language.

Account and support

How do I sign in?

With your Apple Account. No password.

Is it available in my country?

W7SH isn't available in the European Union for now.

Do I have to share my training?

No. Friends, challenges and leaderboards are all optional, and you can report anything abusive.

How do I reach you?

Write to w7sh.app@gmail.com, or use Settings › Help and support in the app.
